## Connected and automated vehicles

My research focuses on cooperative vehicle systems, especially heavy-duty truck and bus platoons. These systems combine vehicle-to-vehicle coordination with onboard decision-making and control to improve operational safety and transport efficiency.

## Predictive and coordinated vehicle control

I develop and study model-based control strategies for longitudinal following, lateral stability, safe vehicle spacing, and trajectory tracking. Key interests include hierarchical nonlinear model predictive control (NMPC), motion coupling, and uncertainty-aware control.

## Energy and emissions optimization

An important objective of my work is to reduce the energy and environmental footprint of commercial transportation. I investigate fuel consumption and NO<sub>x</sub> emissions in diesel powertrains and energy use in electrified vehicles, accounting for road grade, wind, vehicle dynamics, and powertrain behavior.

## Translating research into practice

I am interested in research that connects experimentally validated models, realistic road conditions, and implementation-oriented control design. My work has used observations from cooperative truck platooning trials on Alberta highways.
