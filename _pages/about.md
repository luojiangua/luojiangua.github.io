I am **Luo Jiang**, a researcher and engineer working at the intersection of **connected and automated vehicles**, **intelligent control**, and **energy-efficient transportation**. I received my **Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering from the University of Alberta** in 2026, where I conducted research in the Energy Mechatronics Laboratory under the supervision of **Prof. Mahdi Shahbakhti**.

My doctoral research investigated cooperative control of heavy-duty truck platoons, with particular emphasis on **model predictive control (MPC)**, longitudinal–lateral coordination, fuel-consumption reduction, and NO<sub>x</sub> emissions minimization. My research was informed by measurements from on-road cooperative truck platooning trials on Canadian highways. I currently work on motion planning and control algorithms for automated commercial vehicles.

I am interested in bridging theory and real-world implementation: from vehicle and powertrain modeling, robust and predictive control, and multi-vehicle coordination to deployment-oriented evaluation.

## Research interests

- **Connected and automated vehicles:** cooperative truck and bus platooning, multi-vehicle coordination, and vehicle interaction.
- **Intelligent vehicle control:** model predictive control, longitudinal and lateral motion control, robust control under uncertainty.
- **Energy and emissions optimization:** energy-efficient driving, powertrain-aware control, fuel and hydrogen use, and NO<sub>x</sub> emissions.
- **Autonomous driving:** motion planning, trajectory optimization, and safe operation of commercial vehicles.
