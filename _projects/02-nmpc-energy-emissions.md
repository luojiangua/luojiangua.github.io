## Overview

This research investigates predictive control strategies to maintain cooperative vehicle spacing while improving fuel efficiency and reducing tailpipe NO<sub>x</sub> emissions. Road grade, wind, and powertrain behavior are incorporated into simulation-based evaluations informed by field measurements.

## Key topics

- Hierarchical nonlinear model predictive control for longitudinal platooning.
- Fuel- and emissions-oriented speed and torque optimization.
- Sensitivity to road grade and wind at practical vehicle-following time gaps.
