## Overview

This research addresses joint longitudinal and lateral vehicle control in a heavy-duty truck platoon. The coordinated strategy regulates spacing and vehicle alignment, with the objective of preserving safety and improving energy and emissions performance.

## Key topics

- Longitudinal–lateral coordination of cooperative vehicles.
- Control of spacing and lateral tracking errors.
- Performance evaluation using validated truck models and measured environmental conditions.
