## Overview

An on-road cooperative truck platooning research program studied two SAE Level 2 Class 8 trucks along the Calgary–Edmonton corridor in Canada. Field measurements supported analyses of following behavior, fuel consumption, emissions, and operational performance under winter road conditions.

## Key topics

- On-road cooperative control and safe following at multi-second time gaps.
- Data-driven modeling of heavy-duty truck dynamics and powertrain behavior.
- Analysis of effects from road grade, vehicle loading, and engagement events.

**Related publication:** [Cooperative truck platooning trial on Canadian public highway under commercial operation in winter driving conditions](https://doi.org/10.1177/09544070241245477).
